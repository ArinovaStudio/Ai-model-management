# Skyline Heights Tower A - Commercial Flats & Residential Apartments

## Project Details
Skyline Heights is an ultra-modern 24-storey high-rise situated in Whitefield Tech Corridor, Bengaluru.
Featuring 2BHK and 3BHK premium smart apartments with panoramic skyline views.

## Units & Pricing
- **2BHK Executive Flat**: 1,220 sqft Super Built-up area priced at INR 98,00,000.
- **3BHK Deluxe Flat**: 1,650 sqft Super Built-up area priced at INR 1,35,00,000.
- **Maintenance Deposit**: INR 3.50 per sqft per month for the first 24 months.

## Approvals & Certificates
- **Occupancy Certificate (OC)**: Issued and verified by BBMP.
- **Commencement Certificate (CC)**: Full compliance certificate on record.
- **Fire Safety**: Permanent Fire NOC issued by Karnataka State Fire and Emergency Services.

## Paperwork Checklist for Registration
1. Registered Sale Agreement and Construction Agreement.
2. Occupancy Certificate (OC) copy.
3. Commencement Certificate (CC) copy.
4. Tripartite Agreement with Bank (if opting for home loan).
5. Allotment Letter and Car Parking Allotment Letter.
