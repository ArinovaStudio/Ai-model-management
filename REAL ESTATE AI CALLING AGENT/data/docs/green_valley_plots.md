# Green Valley Residential Enclave - Official Project Specification

## Project Overview
Green Valley Enclave is a premier gated residential plot community situated in North Hills, Bengaluru.
The development covers 35 acres of master-planned layout with 30-foot and 40-foot wide asphalt roads, underground cabling, and landscaped parks.

## Available Plot Inventory & Pricing
- **Standard Plots**: 1,200 sqft (30 ft x 40 ft) priced at INR 48,00,000 (INR 4,000 per sqft).
- **Corner Villa Plots**: 2,400 sqft (40 ft x 60 ft) priced at INR 85,00,000.
- **East-Facing Premium**: 1,500 sqft priced at INR 62,50,000.

## Legal Approvals & Compliance
- **RERA Number**: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/190525
- **Planning Authority**: BDA (Bengaluru Development Authority) sanctioned layout plan.
- **Zoning**: Pure residential conversion with clear parent deeds dating back 30 years.

## Key Landmarks & Surrounding Connectivity
- **Schools & Education**: Located within 10 minutes (4.5 km) of Canadian International School and Stonehill International School.
- **Airport Connectivity**: 25 minutes drive to Kempegowda International Airport via NH 44 expressway.
- **Transit & Roads**: Direct 6-lane access road connecting North Hills main boulevard to Yelahanka and Bellary Road.

## Official Paperwork Provided to Buyers
1. Registered Master Title Deed.
2. Individual A-Katha Certificate and Katha Extract.
3. 15-Year Non-Encumbrance Certificate (Form 15).
4. Deputy Commissioner Land Conversion Order (Agricultural to Residential).
5. Tax clearance certificates and village survey map.
