# Palm Meadows Estates - Signature Luxury Villas

## Project Overview
Palm Meadows Estates is an exclusive 50-acre gated luxury villa community situated on Airport Road, Devanahalli, Bengaluru, just 15 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport.
The enclave features bespoke contemporary 4BHK and 5BHK private pool villas surrounded by landscaped tropical gardens, wide 50-foot tree-lined avenues, and an eco-friendly private lake reservoir.

## Available Villa Inventory & Pricing
- **4BHK Signature Villa**: 3,800 sqft built-up area on a 4,000 sqft plot, priced at INR 3,20,00,000 (INR 3.20 Crores).
- **5BHK Presidential Villa**: 5,200 sqft built-up area on a 6,000 sqft corner plot with private swimming pool, priced at INR 4,85,00,000 (INR 4.85 Crores).
- **Royal Lakeview Villa**: 5,800 sqft built-up area with double-height living room, private elevator, and rooftop solar deck, priced at INR 5,40,00,000 (INR 5.40 Crores).
- **Clubhouse Maintenance Deposit**: INR 4.50 per sqft per month for maintenance of common private roads, landscaping, and 24/7 security patrol.

## Regulatory Approvals & Legal Status
- **RERA Registration**: PRM/KA/RERA/1250/303/PR/210815
- **Planning Authority**: BIAAPA (Bangalore International Airport Area Planning Authority) approved layout and architectural plan sanction.
- **Environmental Clearance**: State Environmental Impact Assessment Authority (SEIAA) Clearance Certificate issued.
- **Airport Authority NOC**: Clear height and flight path NOC obtained from Airports Authority of India (AAI).
- **Title Pedigree**: Freehold clear title verified for 40 consecutive years with complete registered parent deed succession.

## Specifications & Amenities
1. **Interiors**: Italian Botticino marble flooring, double-glazed soundproof German schuco glass windows, and VRV centralized climate control.
2. **Private Amenities**: Private temperature-controlled pool, private solar roof power generation (8 kW system), and smart biometric home automation.
3. **Community Clubhouse**: 25,000 sqft signature clubhouse with all-weather indoor pool, squash court, organic grocery store, and high-speed EV fast-charging stations.
