# Site Visit Protocol, Tour Operations & Customer FAQ

## Tour Scheduling & Operating Hours
- **Operational Days**: Open 7 days a week (Monday through Sunday, including public holidays).
- **Tour Hours**: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.
- **Tour Duration**: Each dedicated site inspection slot is scheduled for 45 to 60 minutes.
- **Last Inspection Slot**: 5:30 PM (to ensure daylight viewing of plots and construction finishes).

## Complimentary Chauffeur & Transit Options
- **Metro Pick-up Service**: Complimentary chauffeur-driven pick-up and drop-off available from the nearest Metro stations (Nagawara for North Hills, Baiyappanahalli for Whitefield, and Silk Board for Sarjapur projects).
- **Direct Site Navigation**: Coordinates, pin locations, and route maps sent via SMS and WhatsApp upon appointment confirmation.
- **Free On-Site Valet**: Secure covered visitor parking and EV charging points available at all project experience centers.

## What to Expect During the Physical Tour
1. **Dedicated Project Specialist**: You will be accompanied by an experienced property advisor who will review layout blueprints, demarcated boundary pegs, and orientation.
2. **Technical & Legal Desk**: On-site civil engineers and legal documentation specialists are available to answer queries on soil tests, foundation depth, and master title deeds.
3. **Model Unit Walkthrough**: For apartments and villas, fully furnished designer experience suites are open for viewing fixtures, layouts, and finishings.

## Virtual & Remote Inspections
- **Live Video Tours**: High-definition WebRTC video walkthroughs available for non-resident Indian (NRI) prospects or remote buyers unable to travel in person.
- **Drone Aerial Footage**: Latest geo-tagged drone mapping available showing access highways, social infrastructure (schools, hospitals, IT parks), and green buffers.

## Rescheduling & Cancellation Policy
- **No Penalty**: Rescheduling is completely free with no cancellation penalties.
- **Notice Period**: Please inform the agent or request a change at least 2 hours before the scheduled appointment so the slot can be released to other prospective buyers.
