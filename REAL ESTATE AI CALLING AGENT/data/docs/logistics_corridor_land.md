# Logistics Corridor Phase 1 - Industrial & Commercial Land Parcels

## Project Overview
Logistics Corridor Phase 1 is a dedicated 120-acre master-planned industrial park situated on the Dabaspete-Nelamangala Highway corridor, Bengaluru West.
Positioned directly on National Highway 48 with direct heavy-freight access to Chennai-Bengaluru Industrial Corridor and Mumbai Highway, designed specifically for grade-A warehousing, supply-chain fulfillment centers, data centers, and manufacturing units.

## Available Land Inventory & Pricing
- **Standard Industrial Plot (2.5 Acres)**: 1,08,900 sqft frontage plot with 60-foot arterial road access, priced at INR 4,50,00,000 (INR 1.80 Crores per acre).
- **Medium Logistics Parcel (5.0 Acres)**: 2,17,800 sqft prime corner land with dual access gates, priced at INR 8,75,00,000 (INR 1.75 Crores per acre).
- **Mega Fulfillment Campus (10.0 Acres)**: 4,35,600 sqft contiguous flat land engineered for high-cube warehousing, priced at INR 16,50,00,000 (INR 1.65 Crores per acre).

## Industrial Infrastructure & Utility Sanctions
- **High-Tension Power Supply**: Dedicated 66/11 kV substation on-site with sanction for up to 1,000 kVA load per unit.
- **Road Network**: 80-foot central spine road with 60-foot feeder roads built with heavy-duty concrete paver blocks rated for 40-foot container trailers.
- **Water & Drainage**: Industrial water pipeline connection from KIADB bulk supply plus 500 KLD Common Effluent Treatment Plant (CETP).
- **Fire Infrastructure**: Dedicated ring main water hydrant network and 24/7 on-campus industrial fire protection system.

## Regulatory Approvals & Legal Status
- **Zoning**: Pure Non-Agricultural (NA) Industrial conversion order sanctioned by the Deputy Commissioner.
- **Industrial Authority**: KIADB (Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board) single-window clearance approved.
- **Pollution Control**: Orange and Green category Consent for Establishment (CFE) issued by KSPCB.
- **RERA Exemption**: Verified commercial-industrial zoning exempt from residential RERA, governed under state industrial act with 99-year leasehold or freehold title options.

## Paperwork Checklist Provided to Purchasers
1. Deputy Commissioner Official Land Conversion Order (Agricultural to NA Industrial).
2. KIADB Single-Window Clearance Allotment Letter.
3. 30-Year Non-Encumbrance Certificate (EC Form 15).
4. Village Survey Map, Podi, and Mutation Register Extract.
5. Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB) Consent for Establishment copy.
6. Electricity Board (BESCOM) Power Feasibility and Sanction Letter.
