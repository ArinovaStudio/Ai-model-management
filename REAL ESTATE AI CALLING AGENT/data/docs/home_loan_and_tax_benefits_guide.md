# Real Estate Financing, Tax Benefits & Registration Cost Guide

## Home Loan Eligibility & Bank Approvals
All residential projects represented by our agency are pre-approved by leading national and private financial institutions:
- **Banking Partners**: State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, and Bank of Baroda.
- **Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to 80% of property agreement value for properties up to INR 75 Lakhs.
  - Up to 75% of property agreement value for properties above INR 75 Lakhs.
- **Required Documentation for Loan Sanction**:
  1. Last 6 months bank account statements showing regular salary credit or business revenue.
  2. Last 3 years Income Tax Returns (ITR) with Computation of Income or Form 16.
  3. KYC documents (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport size photos).
  4. Project Allotment Letter and Draft Sale Agreement provided by the builder.

## Stamp Duty, Registration & Government Fees
When registering property in Karnataka:
- **Stamp Duty**:
  - Properties priced above INR 45 Lakhs: 5% of registered value.
  - Surcharge and Cess: 2% surcharge on stamp duty and 10% cess on stamp duty (approx 5.6% effective stamp duty).
- **Registration Fee**: 1% of property registered agreement value.
- **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)**:
  - Under Section 194-IA of the Income Tax Act, buyer must deduct 1% TDS on total property consideration if property value exceeds INR 50,00,000 (50 Lakhs).
  - TDS must be deposited using Form 26QB within 30 days from the end of the month of transaction.

## Income Tax Deductions for Home Buyers
1. **Section 80C (Principal Repayment)**:
   - Up to INR 1,50,000 (1.5 Lakhs) annual deduction on principal repayment of home loan.
   - Also includes stamp duty and registration charges incurred in the year of purchase.
2. **Section 24(b) (Home Loan Interest)**:
   - Up to INR 2,00,000 (2.0 Lakhs) annual tax exemption on interest paid for self-occupied residential property.
3. **Capital Gains Exemption (Section 54 / 54F)**:
   - Reinvestment of long-term capital gains from sale of previous residential asset into a new residential property grants complete exemption from 20% capital gains tax if completed within 2 years.
