# Emerald Lakeview Townhomes & Row Houses

## Project Overview
Emerald Lakeview is a boutique waterfront gated development consisting of 84 British colonial-style duplex and triplex townhomes situated on the Sarjapur-Bellandur Lake road, Bengaluru East.
Designed for modern IT professionals seeking independent living with community amenities, every row house features a private garden, personal covered car garage, and private terrace.

## Available Inventory & Pricing
- **3BHK Duplex Row House**: 2,150 sqft super built-up area with 400 sqft private garden, priced at INR 1,65,00,000 (INR 1.65 Crores).
- **4BHK Triplex Row House**: 2,750 sqft super built-up area with private terrace garden and family lounge, priced at INR 2,15,00,000 (INR 2.15 Crores).
- **Lakefront Corner Row House**: 3,100 sqft super built-up area overlooking the lake conservation boundary, priced at INR 2,45,00,000 (INR 2.45 Crores).
- **Possession Timeline**: Ready-to-move phase with immediate handover, plus Phase 2 under finishing (handover in December 2026).

## Approvals & Compliance
- **RERA Registration**: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/308/PR/201201
- **Planning Sanction**: BMRDA (Bengaluru Metropolitan Region Development Authority) approved master layout.
- **Lake Buffer Compliance**: Strict 75-meter green buffer zone maintained from lake boundary, certified by Lake Development Authority and NGT compliance report.
- **Bank Pre-Approvals**: Project pre-approved for 80% home loans by State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, and ICICI Bank.

## Key Landmarks & Locality Notes
- **East Bengaluru Waterfront**: Situated specifically in Sarjapur-Bellandur Lake Road, close to RMZ Ecospace and Prestige Tech Park (East corridor).
- **Proximity Distances**: 15 minutes to Outer Ring Road IT corridor; 45 minutes from North Hills / Airport corridor.

## Community Features & Specifications
1. **Clubhouse**: Lakeview infinity pool, badminton court, co-working lounge with high-speed fiber internet, and yoga pavilion.
2. **Eco Infrastructure**: 100% rainwater harvesting, sewage treatment plant (STP) with zero discharge, and dedicated rooftop solar for common lighting.
3. **Security**: Biometric gated entry, RFID boom barriers, perimeter infrared tripwires, and round-the-clock security monitoring.

## Paperwork Checklist for Registration
1. Registered Sale Agreement and Construction Agreement.
2. BMRDA Sanctioned Layout and Building Plan.
3. RERA Certificate and Completion Certificate.
4. Individual BDA/BMRDA Khata Certificate and Khata Extract.
5. Non-Encumbrance Certificate up to current calendar date.
6. NOC from Water Supply Board (BWSSB) and Electricity Board (BESCOM).
