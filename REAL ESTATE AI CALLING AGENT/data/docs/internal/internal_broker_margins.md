## Broker Commission Schedule & Profit Margins
- **In-House Sales Executives**: Commission rate is 2.0% on gross sale value.
- **External Channel Partners**: Tier-1 brokers receive 3.5% commission; referral agents receive 1.5%.
- **Builder Net Margin Structure**:
  - Raw Land Acquisition Cost: INR 1,800 per sqft.
  - Development, Civil, and Approval Cost: INR 1,000 per sqft.
  - Total Builder Cost Basis: INR 2,800 per sqft.
  - Selling Price: INR 4,000 per sqft.
  - Gross Profit Margin: 30.0% (INR 1,200 per sqft).
- **Minimum Floor Price**: Representatives are strictly forbidden from approving any discount below INR 3,850 per sqft without written promoter consent.
