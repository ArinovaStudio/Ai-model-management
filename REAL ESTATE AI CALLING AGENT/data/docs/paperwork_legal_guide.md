# Real Estate Buyer's Paperwork & Due Diligence Guide

## Mandatory Documents for Property Registration
When purchasing any plot or flat through our agency, ensure the following document package is audited:
1. **Title Deed & Chain of Parent Documents**: Verifies uninterrupted ownership for at least 30 consecutive years.
2. **Encumbrance Certificate (EC)**: Reflects whether any mortgages, liens, or legal encumbrances exist against the property.
3. **Katha Certificate & Extract**: Issued by the local revenue authority confirming property registration in the municipal records.
4. **Layout Sanction / Building Sanction Plan**: Proves that the layout or tower was constructed in strict compliance with approved architectural blueprints.
5. **No Objection Certificates (NOCs)**: Issued by Pollution Control, Airport Authority, Electricity Board (BESCOM), and Water Supply Board (BWSSB).
