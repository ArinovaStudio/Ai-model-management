# Metro Hub Commercial Tower - Grade-A Office & Retail Suites

## Project Overview
Metro Hub Commercial Tower is a 16-storey LEED Gold certified commercial landmark located on 100-Ft Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru.
Directly integrated with the Metro Station concourse via an air-conditioned skywalk, offering premium self-contained commercial office suites and ground-floor high-street retail spaces.

## Available Commercial Units & Pricing
- **Executive Boutique Office**: 850 sqft super built-up area, priced at INR 1,25,00,000 (INR 1.25 Crores / INR 14,700 per sqft).
- **Corporate Floor Suite**: 2,400 sqft warm-shell commercial suite with private executive washroom and server room, priced at INR 3,55,00,000 (INR 3.55 Crores).
- **Ground Floor Retail Frontage**: 1,200 sqft triple-height retail showroom facing main arterial traffic, priced at INR 2,90,00,000 (INR 2.90 Crores).
- **Maintenance Deposit**: INR 8.50 per sqft per month including centralized chillers, 100% DG power backup, and building facility management.

## Rental Yields & Investor Returns
- **Projected Rental Yield**: 8.2% to 9.0% gross annual yield for office suites in Indiranagar commercial sub-market.
- **Pre-Leased Options**: Select units available with 5-year and 9-year pre-signed corporate leases with 15% escalation every 3 years.
- **Lock-in Period**: Standard 36-month lease lock-in with multinational and fintech tenants.

## Approvals & Technical Specifications
- **Occupancy Certificate (OC)**: Issued by BBMP (Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike).
- **Green Building Certification**: LEED Gold Certified high-efficiency building with energy-saving curtain-wall glass facade.
- **Power Backup**: 100% redundant diesel generator power backup with synchronized auto-mains failure panels.
- **Vertical Transportation**: 6 high-speed Otis passenger elevators (2.5 m/s) plus dedicated heavy goods/service elevator.
- **Fire Safety**: Complete Fire NOC from Karnataka State Fire Department with automated sprinkler grid and smoke exhaust shafts.

## Legal Checklist Provided to Buyers
1. Registered Commercial Sale Deed and Undivided Share (UDS) allocation.
2. Full Occupancy Certificate (OC) and Commencement Certificate (CC).
3. BBMP Commercial Building Plan Sanction and Khata Extract.
4. Permanent Fire NOC and Airport Height Clearance.
5. Tripartite Lease Management Agreement (for pre-leased investor units).
